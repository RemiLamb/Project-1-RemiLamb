The First Major Zine
Raymond A. Palmer
The first major fanzine to be made was The Comet made by Raymond Palmer and Walter Dennis. It would later be renamed to Cosmology after its fourth issue and it was based around science fiction. It was the official news letter of Science Correspondence Club.
